# Authoritative research sources

Reviewed for the initial C1798 courseware release on 3 August 2026. These are research inputs; learner-facing explanations are original summaries and worked examples.

## Approved course outline

- Tertiary Courses, **Agentic AI for Digital Marketing and Advertising**: <https://www.tertiarycourses.com.sg/agentic-ai-for-digital-marketing-and-advertising.html>
  - Six-topic spine, course identity, 2-day/15-hour duration, audience, and stated tool/application coverage.

## Agent architecture, control, and governance

- Anthropic, **Building Effective AI Agents: Architecture Patterns and Implementation Frameworks**: <https://resources.anthropic.com/hubfs/Building%20Effective%20AI%20Agents-%20Architecture%20Patterns%20and%20Implementation%20Frameworks.pdf>
  - Distinction between predictable workflows and agents; sequential, parallel, evaluator, and orchestrator patterns.
- NIST, **AI Risk Management Framework Core**: <https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/>
  - Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage as continuous risk-management functions.
- NIST, **Generative AI Profile (NIST AI 600-1)**: <https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf>
  - Generative-AI risk considerations, evaluation, documentation, and human oversight.
- Singapore PDPC, **Advisory Guidelines on use of Personal Data in AI Recommendation and Decision Systems**: <https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/guidelines-and-consultation/2024/02/advisory-guidelines-on-use-of-personal-data-in-ai-recommendation-and-decision-systems>
  - Personal-data obligations and consumer transparency for AI systems.
- Advertising Standards Authority of Singapore, **Singapore Code of Advertising Practice**: <https://asas.org.sg/About/Code>
  - Advertisements should be legal, decent, honest, and truthful; claims must not mislead.

## Publishing, measurement, and optimization

- Meta official Postman workspace, **Instagram API documentation**: <https://www.postman.com/meta/instagram/documentation/6yqw8pt/instagram-api>
  - Professional-account requirements, publishing, insights, permissions, and limitations.
- Meta official Postman workspace, **Facebook Marketing API**: <https://www.postman.com/meta/facebook-marketing-api/overview>
  - Campaign operations and insights endpoints for advertising workflows.
- Google Analytics, **Data API dimensions and metrics**: <https://developers.google.com/analytics/devguides/reporting/data/v1/api-schema>
  - Sessions, engagement, events, key events, revenue, and campaign dimensions.
- Google Ads API, **Metrics**: <https://developers.google.com/google-ads/api/fields/v21/metrics>
  - Definitions for clicks, impressions, CTR, average CPC, conversions, cost, and conversion value.
- Google Ads API, **Experiments overview**: <https://developers.google.com/google-ads/api/docs/experiments/overview>

- Google Ads API, **Report on experiments**: <https://developers.google.com/google-ads/api/docs/experiments/reporting>

- Google Ads API, **Optimization score and recommendations**: <https://developers.google.com/google-ads/api/docs/recommendations>
  - Recommendation retrieval and the distinction between suggested and applied changes.
