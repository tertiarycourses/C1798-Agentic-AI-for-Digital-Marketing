# Agentic AI for Digital Marketing and Advertising (C1798) — Hands-On Labs

12 labs across 6 topics · 2 days · 15 scheduled hours

Work through the labs in order — each one builds on the artifacts you produced in the labs before it.


## Setup

Create one local workspace named `aurora-campaign` with these six folders before Lab 1:

```text
aurora-campaign/
  01-foundation/
  02-trends/
  03-content/
  04-launch/
  05-monitor/
  06-optimize/
```

Open every CSV in `labs/resources/` with comma as the delimiter and UTF-8 encoding. Use only the fictional Aurora records; keep all platform and advertising actions in simulation.
