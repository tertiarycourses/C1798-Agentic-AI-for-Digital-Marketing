# Aurora Active — HydraLoop campaign brief

Aurora Active is a fictional Singapore active-lifestyle brand. It is preparing a four-week launch for **HydraLoop**, a reusable insulated water bottle made for commuters, desk workers, and recreational athletes.

## Product facts learners may use

- 750 ml stainless-steel bottle
- Keeps drinks cold for up to 18 hours in the fictional product scenario
- Leak-resistant screw cap and carrying loop
- Available in Ocean Blue, Sunrise Coral, and Forest Green
- Fictional recommended retail price: SGD 48
- Launch offer: 10% off for the first seven days with code `LOOP10`
- Online orders ship within Singapore in the fictional scenario

## Audience segments

1. **Urban commuters, 25-40** — want a bottle that does not leak in a work bag.
2. **Recreational runners, 20-35** — value portability and simple hydration habits.
3. **Office wellbeing coordinators, 30-50** — may buy small team bundles.

## Brand voice

- Energetic, practical, optimistic
- Short sentences and plain English
- Helpful rather than preachy
- Singapore-aware without forced slang
- Never shame people about health or appearance

## Visual direction

- White or pale-sand background
- Ocean blue as primary accent; coral as secondary accent
- Natural daylight, clean shadows, everyday Singapore settings
- Show a realistic product and diverse adults
- Do not imitate a real brand, celebrity, or copyrighted character

## Guardrails

- Do not invent sustainability certifications, medical benefits, testimonials, or awards.
- Treat every performance claim as unverified until a named owner confirms evidence.
- Use only synthetic audience records and comments in class.
- Keep every post, campaign, and budget change in **DRAFT** until a human owner approves it.
- Respect platform rules, personal-data obligations, and the Singapore advertising principle that claims should be legal, decent, honest, and truthful.

## Business objective and starting targets

- Objective: generate qualified product-page visits and first-week purchases.
- Primary KPI: cost per purchase at or below SGD 24 in the fictional paid campaign.
- Secondary KPIs: link CTR of at least 1.5%, landing-page conversion rate of at least 3.5%, and blended ROAS of at least 2.5.
- Guardrails: daily spend cap SGD 300 per platform campaign across its combined ad sets; no audience expansion or budget increase without approval; stop and review if tracking quality, brand safety, or complaint signals deteriorate.
